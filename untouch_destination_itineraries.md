## 1. Jibhi & Shangarh
**Duration:** 4 Days
**Route:** Delhi -> Jibhi -> Jalori Pass -> Shangarh -> Delhi

### Itinerary:
*   **Day 0: Evening Pickup from Delhi** - Depart from Delhi in the evening for Jibhi.
*   **Day 1: Explore Jibhi** - Visit Jibhi Waterfall and Mini Thailand. Enjoy a riverside walk and local cafes. Dinner and bonfire with music at the homestay.
*   **Day 2: Jalori Pass & Raghupur Fort** - Travel to Jalori Pass via Shoja village. Trek to Raghupur Fort (~2.5 hours) for sunset and mountain views. Camping at Jalori Pass.
*   **Day 3: Sunrise & Shangarh** - Witness sunrise at Raghupur Fort. Trek back to Jalori Pass and transfer to Shangarh village to explore the meadows. Homestay in Shangarh.
*   **Day 4: Shangarh & Return to Delhi** - Visit Rupi Raila Waterfall. Begin the return journey to Delhi, arriving the next morning.

*   Pay the remaining 70% on arrival.

**Route:** Delhi -> Kasar Devi -> Munsiyari -> Khaliya Top -> Delhi
