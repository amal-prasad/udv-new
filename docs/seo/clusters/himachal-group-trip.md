## SERP reality check on the seed

`himachal group trip` and `himachal group tour package` both surface the same SERP shape:
Thrillophilia, WanderOn, TravelTriangle, JustWravel, Trekhievers, Swan Tours, D Pauls, Veena
World — large OTA/aggregator listing pages, not single-destination trip pages. This is a
**head term dominated by aggregators with big content-page counts**; matches the brief's
instruction to not chase it directly. Untouch has no realistic shot at page 1 for the bare
seed. Treat it as a category signal, not a target page.

`himachal group trip for solo travellers` and `solo trip to himachal` pull a different,
weaker SERP: TourRadar, Quora, Tripoto trip-journals, Thrillophilia's solo-treks page,
instahimachal blog, TripAdvisor forum threads. Lower competition, more UGC/blog content,
clear opening for a dedicated concept page — confirms the brief's existing mapping to
`/group-trips-for-solo-travellers`.

## Content gaps (biggest to smallest)

1. **Naggar + Parashar Lake as one named trip** — no competitor page combines these; all
   current SERP results treat Parashar/Prashar as a standalone trek from Mandi/Bajaura, not
   paired with Naggar. Clear differentiation opportunity.
2. **"Group trip for solo travellers" + cost/FAQ combined on one page** — current SERP splits
   this into aggregator listings (TourRadar) vs. UGC blogs (Tripoto/Quora); no product page
   answers "how much," "is it safe," "group size" together for Himachal specifically.
2b. **Both "Parashar" and "Prashar" spellings** must appear in on-page copy/meta — SERP
   volume for "Prashar" content is visibly larger than "Parashar."
3. **4-day Zanskar-from-Manali positioning** — every ranking competitor trip is 6+ days;
   Untouch's 4D/3N needs explicit "fastest way to see Zanskar from Manali" framing to win
   the shorter-duration searcher instead of competing head-on with 6-11 day expedition pages.
4. **Zanskar Valley 6-day itinerary** — near-empty SERP for this exact phrase; the Padum
   Circuit page should target it directly with a day-by-day H2 structure (matches what did
   rank: discoverwithdheeraj's Padum sightseeing guide).
5. **Best-time-to-visit and cost content is currently only on generic OTA/blog domains** —
   folding short, honest best-time/cost sections into each trip page (not a separate blog)
   captures this without violating the "no city × destination spam" architecture rule.
