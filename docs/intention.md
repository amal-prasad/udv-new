# Intention

Make udv-new rank for long-tail itinerary searches (`[destination] trip from Delhi`, `[destination] [n] days itinerary`, `[destination] group trip for solo travellers`, `best time to visit [destination]`) and turn that traffic into WhatsApp enquiries.

Untouch Destination is a small-group travel company from Chandigarh running trips across Himachal, Uttarakhand, Ladakh/Zanskar. Booking is WhatsApp only (+91 88008 88489). Audience: Indian travellers 20–40, mobile, mostly from Delhi.

We do not chase head terms ("Himachal tour package") against WanderOn, Thrillophilia, Zostel, IndiaHikes. We win with one real, detailed page per itinerary and per destination, built from one typed data file.

Constraints: keep the calm scroll-stills design; never invent prices, dates, stats or reviews; no extra SEO packages.
